SAP and PKMS

Delivery numbers are matched after trimming spaces, dropping leading zeros, and dropping trailing letters, so SAP 0081175873, PKMS 81175873, 81175873X and 81175873XX are treated as the same delivery. In ORDSTS, when the Pickticket# column has a value (manually entered orders, like control number 29 with Pickticket# 81182054), Pickticket# is used as the delivery number. A delivery that appears more than once in ORDSTS is a redropped order, and the latest drop's status counts. A delivery with more than one ASN (like 81175282 and 81175282X) is received once any ASN is received, and put away only when all of them are. ORDSTS has no invoice date, so outbound invoice dates are worked out by comparing each day's ORDSTS upload with the one before it. Upload ORDSTS every day to keep them exact. Files, notes and that history are saved in this browser only.

How to run MB51 for this scoreboard

  1. Open transaction MB51 (Material Document List).
  2. Material: leave the field empty but set it to not equal to blank. Double-click the empty Material field (or right-click › Options), choose ≠ Not equal to, and confirm. A green ≠ icon appears beside the field, as in the screenshot. This drops lines with no material.
  3. Plant: VB04.
  4. Movement Type: click the multiple-selection arrow to the right of the field and enter 101 and 601. The arrow turns green when more than one value is set.
  5. Posting Date: from far enough back to cover your oldest open PKMS records, through today. Anything in PKMS older than the start date is marked "Can't check" rather than flagged.
  6. Layout: /ESTOQUE C C, or any layout that includes Reference, MvT, Posting Date and Quantity. Reference carries the delivery number.
  7. Execute, then export with List › Export › Spreadsheet and save as .xlsx. Upload it on the SAP MB51 tile.
